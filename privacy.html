NetBench 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 9 日

NetBench 不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人数据，也没有自己的服务器。

本地数据

您的设置（语言、DNS 服务器、公网 IP 服务、IP 信息服务等）以及最近 10 个诊断地址仅保存在设备本地，不会离开您的设备。您可以在最近地址列表中删除单条记录。

本地网络

本地网络权限用于发现局域网设备、Ping、路由追踪、查询本地 DNS 记录和扫描 TCP 端口。所有探测只发往您指定或扫描的目标，结果只在设备上显示。您可以随时在系统设置中关闭此权限。

网络诊断请求

根据您使用的工具，NetBench 会直接向以下对象发出请求：

  • 您输入的目标主机（Ping、路由追踪、端口扫描等）
  • 您选择的 DNS 服务器
  • WHOIS、RDAP 服务（如 whois.iana.org、data.iana.org 及其指向的注册机构）
  • Team Cymru 的 ASN 查询服务（asn.cymru.com）

公网 IP 与 IP 信息

打开「本地网络」时，NetBench 会请求您在设置中选择的公网 IP 服务获取您的公网 IP 地址，再把该地址发送给您选择的 IP 信息服务，查询大致位置和网络运营商。在设置中打开服务列表时，NetBench 会请求列表中的各个服务以测量延迟。

可选的公网 IP 服务：ipify、icanhazip、ident.me、Cloudflare、IP.SB、mir6、DNSPod。

可选的 IP 信息服务：ipinfo、ipwho.is、IP.SB、ipapi.is、GeoJS、mir6。

以上服务由第三方提供，会收到您当前网络的公网 IP 地址，并可能依据其各自的隐私政策保留请求日志。

第三方

本应用不包含任何第三方 SDK、统计分析工具或广告，也不会跟踪您。

NetBench Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

NetBench does not collect, store, or upload any personal data, and has no servers of its own.

Local Data

Your preferences (language, DNS server, public IP service, IP information service, etc.) and up to 10 recent diagnostic addresses are stored only on your device and never leave it. You can remove individual entries from the recent addresses list.

Local Network

Local network access is used to discover devices, ping, trace routes, look up local DNS records, and scan TCP ports. Probes are sent only to the targets you enter or scan, and results are shown only on your device. You can turn this permission off at any time in system settings.

Diagnostic Requests

Depending on the tool you use, NetBench sends requests directly to:

  • The target host you enter (ping, traceroute, port scan, etc.)
  • The DNS server you choose
  • WHOIS and RDAP services (such as whois.iana.org, data.iana.org, and the registries they refer to)
  • Team Cymru's ASN lookup service (asn.cymru.com)

Public IP and IP Information

When you open Local Network, NetBench contacts the public IP service selected in Settings to get your public IP address, then sends that address to the selected IP information service to look up its approximate location and network provider. When you open a service list in Settings, NetBench contacts each service in the list to measure latency.

Available public IP services: ipify, icanhazip, ident.me, Cloudflare, IP.SB, mir6, DNSPod.

Available IP information services: ipinfo, ipwho.is, IP.SB, ipapi.is, GeoJS, mir6.

These services are operated by third parties. They receive your connection's public IP address and may retain request logs under their own privacy policies.

Third Parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising, and does not track you.
